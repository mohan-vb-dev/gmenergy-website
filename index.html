About GM Energy

A Leading Industrial Solutions Provider

GM Energy is an Ahmedabad-based industrial solutions firm engaged in the supply of electrical, mechanical and instrumentation equipment, engineering services, industrial contracting and consultancy.

We support power generation companies and industrial organisations with quality products, dependable services and practical engineering solutions tailored to their operational requirements. With experience in conventional power plants and renewable energy projects, we deliver reliable, cost-effective and technically sound solutions throughout the project lifecycle — from equipment selection and procurement to installation, commissioning and maintenance support.
